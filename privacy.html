Privacy

Plain-language data boundaries.

Effective August 2, 2026. This notice covers the public website, private project intake, Square scheduling and service checkout, and DealerLister fulfillment.

Website analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate page visits and interactions. Google may set or read identifiers according to its own policies and your browser settings. Analytics signals are not treated as verified customer sales.

Private project intake

The project brief stores the name, business, contact information, website, locations, requested service, timeline, and problem you submit so TeeJay's Tech Tools can evaluate fit, prepare requested work, and follow up. Do not submit passwords, full payment-card data, unnecessary customer records, or other sensitive personal information.

Appointments and paid services

Scheduling and checkout are hosted by Square. Square processes booking and payment information under its own terms; TeeJay's Tech Tools does not receive or store full card numbers. TeeJay's Tech Tools stores limited payment and service identifiers needed to confirm paid service orders and prepare delivery.

DealerLister purchases

DealerLister checkout is hosted by Square. The service stores the buyer name, email, plan, payment and order identifiers, purchase amount, fulfillment status, encrypted license material, an expiring setup token, and activation records needed to deliver and support the license.

DealerLister extension data

The extension creates a random installation identifier for seat enforcement and records limited operational event types needed for licensing and support. DealerLister settings and any OpenAI API key you choose to enter are stored in your local Chrome profile. The API key is sent directly to OpenAI when you request AI-generated copy and is not sent to TeeJay's Tech Tools.

Direct communications

If you call, text, email, book, or submit a brief, TeeJay's Tech Tools uses the information to respond, evaluate fit, prepare requested work, maintain business records, and comply with legal obligations. Information is not sold.

Private source material

Client emails, internal messages, source reports, contacts, customer records, and proprietary operating data are not published merely because they were used to understand a business problem. Public case material is anonymized unless a client separately authorizes identification.

Client systems and data

Client work is separately scoped. Access is limited to authorized sources and the agreed purpose. Production, payment, CRM, DNS, customer-facing, and public actions remain under the executive controls defined for the engagement.

Retention and requests

Business communications, intake records, purchases, licenses, activation records, and project records are retained only as reasonably needed for delivery, support, security, accounting, or legal obligations. To request access, correction, or deletion of information you provided, email teejaycrawford@gmail.com.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the website and services change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.
