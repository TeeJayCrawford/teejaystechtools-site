Privacy

Plain-language data boundaries.

Effective August 2, 2026. This notice covers the public TeeJay's Tech Tools website and DealerLister purchase and activation workflow.

Website analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate page visits and interactions. Google may set or read identifiers according to its own policies and your browser settings. Analytics signals are not treated as verified customer sales.

Contact form

The fit-check form runs in your browser and opens your default email application with a draft. The website itself does not receive or store the fields. The information is transmitted only if you choose to send the email, after which it is handled by the email providers involved.

DealerLister purchases

DealerLister checkout is hosted by Square. Square processes payment details under its own privacy terms; TeeJay's Tech Tools does not receive or store full card numbers. The DealerLister service stores the buyer name, email, plan, payment and order identifiers, purchase amount, fulfillment status, encrypted license material, an expiring setup token, and activation records needed to deliver and support the license.

DealerLister extension data

The extension creates a random installation identifier for seat enforcement and records limited operational event types needed for licensing and support. DealerLister settings and any OpenAI API key you choose to enter are stored in your local Chrome profile. The API key is sent directly to OpenAI when you request AI-generated copy and is not sent to TeeJay's Tech Tools. Vehicle details and photos are processed in the browser as part of the user-directed workflow; customers should use only authorized inventory and accounts.

Direct communications

If you call, text, or email, TeeJay's Tech Tools uses the information you provide to respond, evaluate fit, prepare requested work, maintain business records, and comply with legal obligations. Information is not sold.

Client systems and data

Client work is separately scoped. Access is limited to authorized sources and the agreed purpose. Public, customer-facing, production, payment, CRM, and DNS actions remain behind the approval boundary defined for the engagement.

Retention and requests

Business communications, purchase records, licenses, activation records, and project records are retained only as reasonably needed for delivery, support, security, accounting, or legal obligations. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you provided, email teejaycrawford@gmail.com.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the website and services change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.
