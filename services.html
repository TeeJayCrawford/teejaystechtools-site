How we work

A clean boundary around every engagement.

A

Authorized sources

We use only systems and data the client is authorized to provide. Missing coverage is named, not hidden.

B

Defined approval gates

Customer-facing, CRM-changing, production, DNS, payment, and public actions require the agreed human approval.

C

Verified state

Prototype, staged, and public/live are separate states. A deployment is checked at the final destination before it is called live.

D

Measured honestly

Marketing signals are not automatically sales. Economic impact is reported only at the level the source can support.
