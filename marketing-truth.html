What stays clear

  • Which inventory and locations the ads support.
  • How budget is divided and what changed.
  • Which shopper actions are measured.
  • Which pages, feeds, or tracking gaps need attention.

Important boundary

  • An ad click or platform conversion is not automatically a dealership sale.
  • Sales and gross claims require authorized dealer records.
  • Missing CRM, DMS, or accounting coverage stays visible.
  • Campaign changes follow the dealership's approved scope.
