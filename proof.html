Four required labels

A result is not a result until its state is clear.

S

Source

The exact authorized system, document, feed, public page, or record supporting the claim.

C

Coverage

What the source includes, what it misses, and whether the sample can answer the business question.

T

Timestamp

When the evidence was retrieved or verified so old data is never dressed up as current.

V

Verified state

Prototype, local, staged, deployed, delivered, and public/live are separate states with separate checks.
