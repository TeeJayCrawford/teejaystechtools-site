The business logic

One operating system for the whole trailer store.

The customer may be shopping a specific unit, comparing types, arranging financing, renting, ordering, or returning for parts and service. The operation keeps all of those paths connected to the same current inventory and accountable handoffs.

  1. 01Ingest and merchandise the store's current trailer inventory.
  2. 02Publish useful search, VDPs, departments, and lead paths on the live website.
  3. 03Generate and send the eligible current-inventory feed to Google.
  4. 04Manage Google Ads, Vehicle Ads, feed processing, leads, and ongoing site operations as connected but separately verifiable systems.
